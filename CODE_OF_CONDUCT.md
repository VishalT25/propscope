# Code of conduct

Be kind and be useful. This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

- Be respectful. No harassment, personal attacks, slurs or discrimination.
- Assume good faith, and disagree about ideas, not people.
- Keep it on topic. Don't use issues or pull requests to promote gambling services, tips or affiliate links.
- Gambling can hurt people. Don't pressure anyone to bet.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" or open a private security advisory on the repository.
